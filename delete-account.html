Delete your Dextor account

Last updated: 2026-10-26

1. Delete it from the app (recommended)

Sign in to Dextor on Android, Windows, macOS or the web, open the menu, then Settings → Delete account. You will be asked to confirm twice (the second time by typing the word shown on screen), because the action cannot be undone.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you can no longer open the app, send an email to support@dextorsuite.com from the email address of your Dextor account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

Your Dextor account and sign-in link; every patient record, surgery record and setting stored in the cloud for that account; and your license information. If you have an active subscription, its renewal is cancelled automatically so you are not charged again; the paid period is not refunded by deleting the account.

4. What is kept

Billing records (purchase identifier, plan and dates) may be kept for as long as accounting, dispute handling or legal obligations require. Information stored only on your own devices, and backups you exported yourself, are under your control and are not deleted remotely: remove them from your devices if you no longer need them.

5. Subscriptions and refunds

Purchases are made through Google Play. You can also manage or cancel a subscription directly in Google Play at any time. Refund requests are handled by Google Play. See the Privacy Policy for more details.
